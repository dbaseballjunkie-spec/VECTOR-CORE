# VECTOR-CORE

**A deep-research package answering one question:** what game should be built for American
single owner-operators and micro-fleet managers — and exactly what does it look like?

> ## The answer
> **BIG RIG TYCOON** — an **Idle Freight Tycoon**: an idle/incremental business-management
> simulator with a card-draft **load-board** decision layer and asynchronous **convoy** social
> competition. Mobile + browser, 90-second to 20-minute sessions, offline-first, zero forced ads.
>
> **The player** reads a load board, wins a rate in a broker negotiation (*the Handshake*), routes
> the haul against a 14-hour clock, assigns a driver, and reads a clean settlement sheet every
> Friday while the money accrues without him.
>
> **The story** is an AM talk-radio show: eight chapters, one per freight season, an anthology of
> drivers' stories that becomes a single fight against a faceless mega-carrier — ending with the
> only question that matters to a 56-year-old man: *what happens to the name on the door?*
>
> **Why it wins:** card is the highest daily-playtime genre for men aged 40–49; strategy is the
> most age-stable motivation in gaming; idle is the only mechanic that literally mirrors a
> trucker's working life; and the incumbent truck game has 50M+ downloads at **3.8★** — the
> demand exists and the service doesn't.

## The compressed findings

**Study 1 — who he is.** Male (~92%), Gen X, 50–58, married (72%), veteran household (26–36%),
high-school or some college (only ~14% hold a degree), rural South/Midwest (MS 1.57, TN 1.49, TX
1.12 location quotients), nets $60–72k against a $2.26/mile cost and a $1.88/mile rate. Runs 94,000
miles a year. 91.5% of U.S. carriers run ten trucks or fewer; 53% run exactly one.

**Study 1 — what drives him.** Autonomy outranks pay and benefits. He has 3.5-hour dock waits and
fourteen hours of detention a week — time-rich and attention-poor. He reports loneliness at 27.9%
and depression at 26.9%. He is Android-first, offline-constrained, ad-hostile and pay-amenable. He
is numerate, uncredentialed and will not tolerate condescension or dark patterns.

**Study 2 — where he plays.** Card mechanics are the single highest daily-time genre for men his
age (≈26 min/day). Strategy is the most age-proof motivation; competition collapses with age. Idle
games skew older and reward the optimisation he already does for a living. Social casino matches his
demographics perfectly — and was rejected on ethics, not on fit. The incumbent truck game has 50M+
downloads and a 3.8★ rating earned on forced ads and greed.

**Study 3 — the resulting design.** *Idle Freight Tycoon*: idle accrual (money while he drives) +
a card-draft load board (decision density, not RNG) + async convoys (belonging without obligation)
+ authenticity as the moat. One game, deep, with the edge cases as expansion layers — plus one free
daily puzzle as the acquisition funnel.
